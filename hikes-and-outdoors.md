# 🥾 Hikes & Outdoors — Skaneateles June 2026

> Base: 1557 Red Tail Ln, Skaneateles NY (on the lake)

## Very Close (< 20 min)

### Carpenter Falls / Bahar Nature Preserve ⭐
- **Distance:** 1.7 miles total; ~0.4 mi to falls view
- **Difficulty:** Easy–Moderate (431 ft elevation loss to lake shore)
- **Highlights:** 90-foot waterfall on Skaneateles Lake — jaw-dropping in June with high water. Secondary "Angel Falls" lower on trail. 65 ft of lakeshore access — can kayak in.
- **Toddler verdict:** Manageable in a carrier; partial flat trail to upper falls view. Skip the full descent to shore with a toddler.
- **Parking:** Small paved lot at junction of Appletree Point Rd & Carver Rd, Niles, NY (~12 cars). Arrive early on weekends.
- **Address:** Appletree Point Rd, Town of Niles, Cayuga County (west side of lake, south of village ~15 min)
- **Info:** [Finger Lakes Land Trust](https://www.fllt.org/preserves/bahar-preserve-carpenters-falls)

---

### Skaneateles Conservation Area (Guppy Falls) 🐣
- **Distance:** Varies (multiple loops 0.5–3+ miles)
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Highlights:** 25-foot Guppy Falls, pond, observation deck, shaded forest. Town-maintained trails with a good trail map.
- **Toddler verdict:** Great — short, flat, and interesting. Best intro hike for Egan.
- **Parking:** Main lot off Old Seneca Turnpike; GPS: N42 58.085 W76 23.409 (Guppy Falls trailhead)
- **Address:** Town of Skaneateles — ask at Town Hall or check [townofskaneateles.gov](https://www.townofskaneateles.gov) for trail map

---

### Charlie Major Nature Trail
- **Distance:** ~2.5 miles
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Highlights:** Shaded woodland loop, popular with locals and dogs, good birding in June
- **Toddler verdict:** Easy terrain, mostly flat
- **Address:** Off Route 321, Skaneateles area

---

### Bear Swamp State Forest
- **Distance:** Extensive network (multiple trails)
- **Difficulty:** Easy–Moderate
- **Highlights:** South of Skaneateles Lake, this forest is literally the watershed buffer for the lake. Deep hemlock and mixed hardwood. Quiet and beautiful.
- **Address:** Onondaga County south/Cortland County border, Town of Scott area

---

### Baltimore Woods Nature Center
- **Distance:** Trail network through 180+ acres
- **Difficulty:** Easy–Moderate
- **Highlights:** Forest, meadows, and ponds. Trails open year-round; programs available (most age 5+). Good place for a stroller-friendly walk with the toddler. Weekly free **Naturalist's Bumble** for adults Wed 9am.
- **Toddler verdict:** Easy-to-moderate trails work for stroller hikes; nature center building has program calendar for older kids.
- **Address:** 4007 Bishop Hill Rd, Marcellus, NY · ~15 min from Skaneateles

---

### Hinchcliff Family / High Vista / Ripley Hill Preserves
- **Distance:** ~2+ miles combined
- **Difficulty:** Easy–Moderate
- **Highlights:** Three connected Finger Lakes Land Trust preserves on the southeast rim of Skaneateles Lake — some of the best **lake views** in the area
- **Address:** East side of Skaneateles Lake, Onondaga County

## 20–35 Minutes Away

### Green Lakes State Park ⭐
- **Distance from Skaneateles:** ~20–25 min (Fayetteville)
- **Trails:**
  - Green Lake Loop: **2 miles**, easy–moderate, ~50 min
  - Figure-8 (both Green + Round Lakes): **~3.3 miles**, moderate
  - Full Perimeter Trail: **9.9 miles**, moderate (990 ft gain)
  - Vista Trail: bonus views to Tug Hill Plateau, Oneida Lake, Lake Ontario
- **Highlights:** Two rare meromictic lakes with stunning turquoise water (similar chemistry to the Dead Sea — layers don't mix). Sandy swimming beach. Old-growth forest section off Round Lake. Disc golf and traditional golf courses also on site.
- **Toddler verdict:** Lake loops are very walkable; paved sections near picnic area. Sandy beach is a bonus.
- **Parking:** Green Lakes Park Dr, Fayetteville, NY 13066
- **Fee:** Empire Pass or day-use fee (~$7–10/vehicle)
- **Info:** [cnyhiking.com](https://cnyhiking.com/NYSP-GreenLakes.htm)

---

### Clark Reservation State Park
- **Distance from Skaneateles:** ~25 min (Jamesville)
- **Distance:** ~2 mile loop around Glacier Lake
- **Difficulty:** Moderate–Rugged (wild, rocky trails)
- **Highlights:** Glacier Lake — meromictic like Green Lakes but wilder and more rugged. Nature center open Memorial Day–Labor Day. Hart's fern habitat.
- **Toddler verdict:** Rougher terrain; manageable in a good carrier but not stroller-friendly
- **Parking:** 6105 E Apulia Rd, Jamesville, NY 13078
- **Fee:** Empire Pass or day-use fee

---

### Tinker Falls (Labrador Hollow Unique Area) ⭐
- **Distance from Skaneateles:** ~30–35 min (Tully/Apulia)
- **Distance:** 0.4 miles to falls (0.8 miles round trip) — or extend to Jones Hill Vista (1.1 mi one-way, 664 ft gain)
- **Difficulty:** Easy to falls; Moderate for Jones Hill
- **Highlights:** You walk *behind* the waterfall along a stone ledge — magical and unusual. June water flow is excellent. Labrador Hollow is a quiet glacial valley.
- **Toddler verdict:** Walk to the falls is super easy and short. Perfect for Egan — dramatic payoff for minimal effort.
- **Parking:** Route 91, south of Apulia, Onondaga/Cortland County line (~15 cars in lot)
- **GPS:** 42.78012°N, 76.04xxx°W (see AllTrails)
- **Info:** [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-york/tinkers-falls-trail)

## 40–45 Minutes Away

### Fillmore Glen State Park ⭐
- **Distance from Skaneateles:** ~40 min (Moravia)
- **Trails:**
  - Gorge Trail: ~2.5 miles, Intermediate (multiple waterfalls, 9 stone bridges)
  - South Rim + Gorge Loop: **2.82 miles**, Moderate
  - Full 5-mile Loop (N/S Rim + Gorge): Moderate
- **Highlights:** Multiple waterfalls through a dramatic gorge. First waterfall ("Cowsheds Falls") is **wheelchair accessible**. Swimming area in park. June is peak waterfall season.
- **Toddler verdict:** First waterfall accessible with stroller. Going deeper requires carrying Egan.
- **Parking / Address:** 1686 NY-38, Moravia, NY 13118 | (607) 842-6820
- **Fee:** Empire Pass or day-use fee

---

### Taughannock Falls State Park ⭐⭐
- **Distance from Skaneateles:** ~45 min (Trumansburg/Ulysses)
- **Trails:**
  - Gorge Trail: **3 miles round-trip**, Easy — flat path to base of falls
  - North & South Rim Trails: 1.5 mi each, scenic overlooks
  - Full Loop: ~4.3 miles, Moderate
- **Highlights:** **215-foot plunge waterfall** — taller than Niagara, one of the highest east of the Rockies. Gorge trail is flat and easy (great for toddlers in a carrier or walking). Spectacular in June.
- **Toddler verdict:** Gorge trail is the most toddler-friendly major waterfall hike in the region — flat, wide, paved.
- **Parking / Address:** 2221 Taughannock Rd (NY-89), Trumansburg, NY 14886 | (607) 387-6739
- **Fee:** Empire Pass or day-use fee

---

### Robert H. Treman State Park (Lucifer Falls)
- **Distance from Skaneateles:** ~45 min (south Ithaca)
- **Trails:**
  - Lucifer Falls via Gorge & Rim Trail: **4.6-mile loop**, Moderate (popular AllTrails #1)
  - Gorge Trail alone (to Lucifer Falls): ~2 miles one-way
- **Highlights:** 115-foot Lucifer Falls, wild Devil's Kitchen gorge, stone stairways. Natural swimming hole at Lower Falls (huge draw in summer). Old grist mill.
- **Toddler verdict:** Gorge trail has significant steps and wet rocks — bring a good carrier. Swimming hole makes it very kid-friendly if you can get there.
- **Address:** 105 Enfield Falls Rd, Ithaca, NY 14850 | (607) 273-3440
- **Fee:** Empire Pass or day-use fee

---

### Buttermilk Falls State Park (Ithaca)
- **Distance from Skaneateles:** ~45 min
- **Trails:**
  - Gorge Trail: ~1.9 miles, Moderate (steep stone stairs)
  - Rim Trail: parallel, easier return
  - Full Loop via Gorge + Rim: **~2.5 miles**
- **Highlights:** 165-foot main falls, natural swimming hole at base (massive summer draw). Multiple cascades on the way up. Lake Treman at top.
- **Toddler verdict:** Swimming hole at base is great; gorge trail involves steps, best with carrier
- **Address:** 112 E Buttermilk Falls Rd, Ithaca, NY 14850 | (607) 273-5761
- **Fee:** Empire Pass or day-use fee

## Bike & Rail Trails

### Skaneateles Creek Trail
- **Length:** 0.8 miles
- **Type:** Rail-to-trail, flat
- **Highlights:** Former Skaneateles Short Line Railroad corridor along Skaneateles Creek. Remnants of old railroad and water-powered industries. Playground at Mottville Trailhead (north end) — great for Egan.
- **Trailhead:** Mottville Trailhead off Crow Hill Rd; also accessible from village

### Old Erie Canal State Historic Park
- **Length:** ~36 miles (Dewitt to Rome)
- **Type:** Flat canal towpath / multi-use trail
- **Highlights:** Historic canal with original stonework, locks, and aqueducts. Very flat — great for bikes with a trailer. Located near Fayetteville/Chittenango (~25 min from Skaneateles, near Green Lakes).
- **Info:** parks.ny.gov

### Charlie Major Trail
- **Length:** ~2.5 miles
- **Type:** Natural surface, multi-use
- Good for easy trail running or walking

## Running Routes in/around Skaneateles Village

- **Clift Park Waterfront Path** — flat lakeside loop, beautiful views, 1+ miles
- **Skaneateles Creek Trail** — 0.8 mi flat former rail corridor from village south
- **Village to Lake roads** — rolling terrain, scenic lakeside neighborhoods
- **Skaneateles Conservation Area trails** — off-road running, varied terrain
- **Route 41A south along the lake** — road run with lake views on the west shore

## Manual Follow-Ups

- [ ] **State Park day-use fees:** Confirm 2026 rates. Empire Pass (~$80/yr) pays for itself after 3 visits if hitting multiple parks. Buy at [parks.ny.gov](https://parks.ny.gov).
- [ ] **Carpenter Falls parking:** Only ~12 spots. Arrive by 8am on weekends in June.
- [ ] **Tinker Falls trail conditions:** After heavy rain the walk-behind ledge can be slippery — check ahead.
- [ ] **Taughannock Rim Trails:** North/South Rim trails can be muddy in early June — check conditions before bringing Egan.
- [ ] **Fillmore Glen gorge trail:** As of 2025, part of trail was closed (see Instagram reports) — verify current status before heading out.
- [ ] **Skaneateles Conservation Area trail map:** Download from [townofskaneateles.gov](https://www.townofskaneateles.gov) before the trip.
- [ ] **AllTrails:** Download Green Lakes and Taughannock trails offline before leaving — cell service in gorges is spotty.
