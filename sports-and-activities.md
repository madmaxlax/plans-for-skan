## 🏌️ Golf

### Pearl Lakes Golf Course & Driving Range
**Closest course to the village — includes driving range**

- **Address:** 1441 Old Seneca Turnpike, Skaneateles, NY 13152
- **Distance:** ~4 miles from village center
- **Type:** Public | 9-hole executive course (par 29) + driving range
- **Green Fees:** ~$15 / 18 holes equivalent (executive 9-holer, very affordable)
- **Phone:** (315) 685-6799
- **Email:** pearllakesgc@gmail.com
- **Website:** pearl-lakes-golf-course.business.site
- **Hours:** Open to visitors every day
- **Notes:** The most convenient option — right in Skaneateles. Good warm-up/casual round + easy range sessions.

### Dutch Hollow Country Club
**Scenic 18-hole between Skaneateles and Owasco Lakes**

- **Address:** 1839 County Road 117, Owasco, NY 13021
- **Distance:** ~8 miles from Skaneateles
- **Type:** Public (semi-private) | 18 holes, par 71, 6,527 yards (blue tees)
- **Green Fees:** ~$28–$42 (varies by time; check GolfNow for deals)
- **Booking:** golfnow.com (search "Dutch Hollow") or call the pro shop
- **Website:** Listed on GolfNow and GolfPass
- **Dress Code:** No tee shirts, tank tops, cut-off shorts, or gym shorts
- **Notes:** Well-reviewed conditions. Situated on a ridge between Skaneateles and Owasco Lakes. Popular with local golfers from Skaneateles, Camillus, and Auburn.

### Lakeview Golf & Country Club
**Historic Bendelow-designed 18-hole, semi-private near Auburn**

- **Address:** ~7 miles from Skaneateles, ~2 miles from Auburn (near Owasco Lake)
- **Distance:** ~15 minutes from Skaneateles
- **Type:** Semi-private/public | 18 holes, par 70, 6,564 yards
- **Green Fees:** ~$30–$40 (weekday/weekend); cart additional
- **Booking:** foreupsoftware.com (online tee times)
- **Notes:** Designed by Tom Bendelow in 1916. Historic course with character. Tee times available starting 12pm.

### Tanner Valley Golf Course

## 🏓 Pickleball

### Austin Park Outdoor Courts
**Free, drop-in, outdoor**

- **Address:** State St, Skaneateles, NY 13152
- **Courts:** 4 outdoor asphalt courts with permanent pickleball lines

## 🏊 Swimming & Triathlons

### Clift Park Waterfront — Open Water on Skaneateles Lake
**Free public swimming in one of the cleanest lakes in the US**

- **Address:** Clift Park, Village of Skaneateles (downtown, lakeside)
- **Open:** End of June – end of August
- **Cost:** Free public access
- **Notes:** Skaneateles Lake is among the cleanest lakes in the country (it supplies drinking water to Syracuse without filtration). Perfect for open-water swim training. The lake stretches 16 miles — kayak/paddleboard alongside your swim for safety.

---
