## Fine Dining / Upscale (Reserve Ahead!)

### Sherwood Inn ⭐
- **Address:** 26 W Genesee St, Skaneateles, NY 13152
- **Phone:** (315) 685-3405 | [sherwoodinns.com](https://sherwoodinns.com)
- **Cuisine:** American / Lakeside fine dining
- **Price:** $$$ – $$$$
- **Meals:** Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
- **Highlights:** Historic 1807 inn directly across from Clift Park. Beautiful dining room with antique furnishings. Known for classic American fare, excellent brunch, and their signature Fish Fry for Two on Fridays (loaf of Patisserie bread, New England chowder, dessert for $40). Lakeside ambience is unbeatable.
- **Toddler notes:** Upscale setting but not unfriendly to families — best for breakfast or lunch with Egan rather than dinner. High chairs available.
- **Don't miss:** The attached **Patisserie** bakery shop (open daily) — rotating artisan pastries, croissants, scones. Must-stop every visit.

### Rosalie's Cucina
- **Address:** 841 W Genesee St, Skaneateles, NY 13152
- **Phone:** (315) 685-2200 | [rosaliescucina.com](https://rosaliescucina.com)
- **Cuisine:** Italian / Contemporary American
- **Price:** $$$
- **Meals:** Dinner
- **Highlights:** Rustic Italian, wood-fired dishes, handmade pasta, great wine list. Lively and popular — one of the most beloved restaurants in the village.
- **Toddler notes:** Relaxed Italian atmosphere — manageable with a toddler but best saved for a date night or adults-only dinner. Not a kid-specific menu.

## Mid-Range (Great for Groups / Mixed Toddler/Adult)

### Bluewater Grill ⭐
- **Address:** 11 W Genesee St, Skaneateles, NY 13152
- **Phone:** (315) 685-6600
- **Cuisine:** Seafood / American
- **Price:** $$–$$$
- **Meals:** Lunch, Dinner
- **Highlights:** Great outdoor patio with lake views. Seafood-forward menu — fish, lobster, clam chowder — plus burgers, chicken, pasta. One of the best spots for a casual-but-nice lakeside meal. Strong cocktail menu.

## Casual & Kid-Friendly 🐣

### Doug's Fish Fry ⭐⭐⭐ (The Classic Skaneateles Institution)
- **Address:** 8 Jordan St, Skaneateles, NY 13152
- **Phone:** (315) 685-3288 | [dougsfishfryskaneateles.com](https://dougsfishfryskaneateles.com)
- **Cuisine:** Fried seafood, casual American
- **Price:** $–$$
- **Hours:** Check for current season hours (typically lunch through dinner)
- **Highlights:** Legendary Skaneateles spot since forever. Counter-service fried seafood — haddock, shrimp, clams, grilled options, chowder. Fish sandwich ~$10, fish dinner ~$14. Beer & wine available. Also serves ice cream and shakes.
- **Toddler notes:** Made for this — counter service, outdoor seating, zero formality. Egan can make a mess and no one cares. *Don't leave Skaneateles without coming here.*

## Auburn Day-Trip Lunch Options

### Prison City Pub & Brewery ⭐
- **Address:** 28 State St, Auburn, NY 13021
- **Phone:** (315) 406-2494 | [prisoncitypubandbrewery.com](https://prisoncitypubandbrewery.com)
- **Price:** $$
- **Highlights:** Best craft brewery in Auburn. Great pub food (burgers, wings, fish tacos), 20+ taps of house beers, excellent atmosphere. Named after Auburn's historic Auburn Correctional Facility (now a working prison).
- **Toddler notes:** Casual, pub atmosphere — manageable with a toddler for lunch

### Elderberry Pond Restaurant ⭐
- **Address:** 3576 Center St Rd, Auburn, NY 13021
- **Price:** $$–$$$
- **Highlights:** Farm-to-table dining on a working organic farm. Beautiful setting, seasonal menu, excellent local sourcing. More of an experience than a quick stop.
- **Toddler notes:** Outdoor/garden setting great for curious toddlers

- Great if you're pairing an Auburn day with Fillmore Glen hike
