## Fishing 🎣

### What's Biting in June

| Species | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Smallmouth Bass** | Excellent throughout the lake. ⚠️ Catch-and-release only until ~June 15 — verify 2026 NYS regs |
| **Largemouth Bass** | North and south shallower ends |
| **Walleye** | North/south warm-water ends; evening and dawn bites |
| **Lake Trout** | Deep cold water (mid-lake); trolling |
| **Rainbow Trout / Landlocked Salmon** | Stocked; spring/summer trolling |
| **Pickerel** | South end and weedy shallows |
| **Panfish / Bullhead / Perch** | South end near Fuller Park; good for kids |

> **Best June strategy:** Early morning topwater for bass near shoreline structure; troll for lake trout mid-lake; fish the south end flats for warm-water species

### NYS Fishing License
- **Required** for anyone 16 and older
- Purchase online before the trip: [dec.ny.gov/permits/fish-hunt-trap/fishing](https://dec.ny.gov/permits/fish-hunt-trap/fishing)
- Also available at: Skaneateles Marina (bait + licenses), local sporting goods stores
- Bait available at Skaneateles Marina

### Good Public Fishing Spots
- **NYS Public Boat Launch (Route 41A):** ~2.5 miles south of village on west shore; cold water area, good for trout/salmon trolling
- **Andrew R. Fuller Park (south end):** Panfish, bullhead, pickerel, bass
- **Pine Grove area (~2 mi south of village, west side):** Free launch; "between the points" rule of thumb for Finger Lakes fishing
- **Village pier at Clift Park:** Light fishing permitted from pier

## Swimming & Public Beach Access 🏊

### Your Rental (Best Option for Egan!)
- **1557 Red Tail Ln** — direct Skaneateles Lake access
- Check rental listing for dock, launch, and swimming conditions
- Private lake access = safest and easiest for a 2-year-old in a life jacket
- Early mornings are calm and gorgeous on the lake

### Clift Park Swimming Area (Village)
- **Address:** W Genesee St, Skaneateles (across from Sherwood Inn)
- **Open:** Late June through end of August
- **Lifeguards:** Daily 11am–7pm (weather permitting); **NO swimming without lifeguards on duty**
- **Fee:** Non-residents pay daily admission; free with town resident season pass
- **Water:** Stunning Caribbean-blue clarity — Skaneateles is one of the cleanest lakes in the US
- **Rules:**
  - Children under 7 must be within arm's reach of adult at all times
  - No recreational flotation devices in swim area (no noodles, tubes)
  - Swim test required to go past shallow area
  - No dogs allowed in park
- **Note:** Swimming area typically opens **late June** — may not be open first days of a June trip

### Other Swimming Spots
- **Andrew R. Fuller Park (south end):** Informal swimming access near boat launch
- **Bahar Nature Preserve:** 65 ft of lakeshore at trail end — good for a dip after the Carpenter Falls hike
- **NYS boat launch areas:** Can access the water for swimming, no lifeguards

## Toddler Water Safety Notes 🧡

- **Life jackets:** Make sure Egan has a properly fitted USCG-approved infant/toddler PFD (Type II or III, ≤30 lbs or 30–50 lbs as needed). Many rentals include adult sizes but not infant — **bring your own.**
- **Kayak with toddler:** Sit-on-top kayaks are more stable and easier for getting in/out with a toddler
- **Pontoon boats:** Best option for a family outing — stable platform, railings, plenty of space
- **Sightseeing cruise:** Dogs welcome, toddler-friendly, captain + crew onboard — safest intro to the lake
