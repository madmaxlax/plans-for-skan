## 🏛️ Museums

### MOST — Milton J. Rubenstein Museum of Science & Technology ⭐ (best for Egan)
- **Address:** 500 S Franklin St, Syracuse, NY 13202 (~30 min)
- **Phone:** (315) 425-9068
- **Website:** most.org
- **Hours:** Thu–Sun 10am–5pm (plus Mon/Tue during school breaks)
- **Admission:** ~$10/adult; children under 2 **free** for general admission (verify at visit)
- **Why go:** The 5-level **Science Playhouse** is the standout for toddlers — sensory-friendly, hands-on exploration across all levels. Sunday family events, storytime, and STEM activities included. Best museum pick for Egan's age.

### Schweinfurth Memorial Art Center — Auburn ⭐
- **Address:** 205 Genesee St, Auburn, NY 13021 (~15 min)
- **Phone:** (315) 255-1553
- **Website:** schweinfurthartcenter.org
- **Hours:** Wed–Sat 10am–5pm · Sun 1–5pm · Closed Mon/Tue
- **Admission:** $10/adult · **Free for children 12 & under** · $15 joint admission with Cayuga Museum
- **Why go:** Rotating gallery exhibitions (fine art, quilts, fiber arts, regional artists). Easy, low-key, Egan can wander through galleries without chaos. **First Fridays: free admission** all day. Co-located with Cayuga Museum of History & Art — both on the West End Arts Campus.

### Erie Canal Museum — Syracuse
- **Address:** 318 Erie Blvd E, Syracuse, NY 13202 (~30 min)
- **Phone:** (315) 471-0593
- **Website:** eriecanalmuseum.org
- **Hours:** Typically Mon–Sat 10am–5pm, Sun noon–5pm (call to confirm)
- **Admission:** ~$5/adult; kids often free
- **Why go:** Only remaining weighlock structure in NY; life-size canal boat you can walk through; interactive Erie Canal history exhibits. Good for a quick 1-hour stop combined with another Syracuse activity.

### Cayuga Museum of History & Art — Auburn
- **Address:** 203 Genesee St, Auburn, NY 13021 (~15 min) — same campus as Schweinfurth
- **Phone:** (315) 253-8051
- **Website:** cayugamuseum.org
- **Hours:** Wed–Sat 10am–5pm · Sun 1–5pm
- **Admission:** $10 · Joint ticket with Schweinfurth: $15
- **Why go:** NY history, Cayuga Nation exhibits, Theodore Case sound-film collection. Good adult/history interest pairing with Schweinfurth.

## 🏠 At the House — Rainy Day Activities

### 👶 With Egan (2-Year-Old Activities)

1. **Sensory Bin** — Fill a bin with dry rice, pasta, or beans + spoons/cups/small toys. Let Egan dig, pour, and transfer. 30–45 minutes of occupation, guaranteed.

2. **Baking Soda Volcano** — Spoon baking soda into a cup, pour white vinegar, watch it fizz. He'll ask to do it 20 times in a row. Buy the big box.

3. **Finger Painting** — Tape paper to the table (or a trash bag on the floor). Washable tempera paint. No instruction needed — just let him go.

4. **Fort Building** — Blankets over chairs and a couch. Crawl inside with a flashlight and stuffed animals. Add a "secret knock."

5. **Dance Party** — Queue up a toddler playlist (Raffi, They Might Be Giants' *Here Comes the ABCs*, Kidz Bop). Clear the living room floor. Go hard.

6. **Water Pouring Station** — Fill a bin in the bathtub with cups, funnels, pitchers. Egan pours and transfers water indefinitely. High ROI.

7. **Pasta Threading / Lacing** — Giant tubular pasta (rigatoni, penne) and a shoelace or pipe cleaners. Threading practice doubles as toddler focus activity.

8. **Shaving Cream Sensory Play** — Squirt a small pile of shaving cream on a tray or in the bathtub. Let him squish, draw lines in it, make "soup." Rinse clean.

9. **Indoor Obstacle Course** — Couch cushions to crawl over, a pillow tunnel, painter's tape hopscotch on the floor. Name each station.

10. **Play Dough + Tools** — Use cookie cutters, forks, rolling pins. If you don't have play dough: mix 2 cups flour, 1 cup salt, 1 cup water, 2 tbsp oil + food coloring.

11. **Kitchen Music Band** — Wooden spoons, pots, Tupperware lids as cymbals, an oatmeal container drum. Perform a concert.

12. **Bubble Bonanza (covered porch)** — If there's a covered porch, blow bubbles in the rain. Toddlers will lose their minds.

13. **Sticker Book / Sticker Wall** — Give Egan a giant roll of stickers and a piece of contact paper taped to the wall. He'll spend 20 minutes placing and rearranging stickers.

14. **Flashlight Hide & Seek** — Turn the lights off, give Egan a flashlight, hide stuffed animals around the room for him to find.

15. **Car Wash for Toy Trucks** — Set up a bin of soapy water and let Egan "wash" his toy cars and trucks. Works best on a tile floor or in the bathtub.
