## Day Trips

### Auburn (~15 min west on US-20) 🏛️

A must-do day — close, rich history, and Owasco Lake waterfront.

**Harriet Tubman National Historical Park** ⭐
- 180 South St, Auburn, NY 13021
- **Hours:** Fri–Sat, 10am–4pm (seasonal; verify 2026 summer hours)
- Ranger-guided tours at 11:30am and 3:00pm
- Includes Thompson Memorial A.M.E. Zion Church (where Tubman worshipped and was buried from) and the Parsonage visitor center
- **Admission:** Free (National Park Service)
- [nps.gov/hart](https://nps.gov/hart)

**Harriet Tubman Home (separate from NHP)**
- 180 South St, Auburn NY
- Managed by the A.M.E. Zion Church — the actual homestead grounds
- Call ahead for tour availability: (315) 252-2081

**Seward House Museum**
- 33 South St, Auburn, NY 13021
- Historic home of William H. Seward (Lincoln's Secretary of State; "Seward's Folly" = Alaska purchase)
- (315) 252-1283 | [sewardhouse.org](https://sewardhouse.org)

**Schweinfurth Art Center**
- 205 Genesee St, Auburn, NY 13021
- Regional art museum with rotating exhibits
- (315) 255-1553

**Emerson Park & Owasco Lake Waterfront**
- North end of Owasco Lake (adjacent to Auburn)
- Picnic pavilions, boat launches, playground — excellent with Egan
- Free admission to park; nominal fees for pavilions

**Cayuga County Fair (check dates)**
- Traditionally July in Auburn — verify 2026 schedule

### Ithaca day trip (~45 min south) 🏞️

Best day trip for waterfall-and-nature lovers. Combine with gorge hiking (see hikes file). Roll multiple stops into one day:

- **Robert H. Treman State Park** — 105 Enfield Falls Rd. Lucifer Falls, swimming hole at Lower Falls, gorge trail.
- **Buttermilk Falls State Park** — 112 E Buttermilk Falls Rd. Swimming under the falls (natural pool), gorge trail.
- **Taughannock Falls State Park** — 2221 Taughannock Rd, Trumansburg (~35 min). Easiest waterfall hike for Egan.
- **Ithaca Commons** — East State St pedestrian mall: shopping, dining, street performers.
- **Ithaca Farmers Market** — 545 3rd St (Steamboat Landing). Sat 9am–3pm, Sun 10am–3pm (May–Dec). One of the best in upstate NY — right on the water.
- **Cascadilla Gorge** — Free in-town walk from downtown to Cornell campus.
- **Cornell University Campus** — Free to walk; Herbert F. Johnson Museum of Art (free).

> **Strategy:** Drive down for 9am Sat farmers market → Buttermilk Falls swim → lunch on Commons → drive home. Or hit Taughannock in the morning for easy toddler hike, then lunch in Trumansburg/Ithaca.

### Marcellus & Around (~15–20 min) 🌿

**Baltimore Woods Nature Center**
- 4007 Bishop Hill Rd, Marcellus, NY · ~15 min
- Trails open year-round; 180+ acres of forest, meadows, and ponds
- Program calendar runs all summer — most kid programs are 5+ (limited toddler appeal for Egan)
- Weekly **Naturalist's Bumble** — Wednesdays 9am, free, adults: a perfect Max-solo morning walk
- Telescope workshops + family field days throughout summer

**Crazy Daisies Greenhouse & Garden Cafe**
- 4693 Kasson Rd, Syracuse, NY 13215 · ~20 min
- Family + dog-friendly greenhouse with garden cafe — "a feast for the senses"
- Frequent live music, trivia nights (Thu + Sun), pilates in the garden, plant workshops
- Open daily
- Good toddler stroll for Egan; adults get good food + events
- https://crazydaisiesflowers.com

**Prison City Brewing — The Farm (North Road)** ⭐
- 251 North Street, Auburn, NY · ~20 min
- The farm/taproom location of Prison City Brewing (different from their downtown Auburn pub at 28 State St)
- Outdoor farm setting — great summer evening vibe, dog and family friendly
- **Live music Fridays 6–9pm** (Summer Music at the Farm series, May–September 2026)
- **Live music Sundays 2–5pm** (Sunday afternoon sessions, same series)
- **Trivia Thursdays 6:30pm**
- Craft beer + outdoor hang — a go-to for summer evenings when you want something livelier than the village but don't want a full dinner out
- [prisoncitybrewing.com](https://prisoncitybrewing.com)

### Seneca Falls (~35 min west) 🗽

**Women's Rights National Historical Park**
- 136 Fall St, Seneca Falls, NY 13148
- Site of the 1848 Women's Rights Convention; the original Declaration of Sentiments
- **Admission:** Free (NPS)
- [nps.gov/wori](https://nps.gov/wori)

**It's a Wonderful Life Connection**
- Seneca Falls is widely believed to be the inspiration for Bedford Falls in the movie
- "It's a Wonderful Life" Museum: 32 Fall St, Seneca Falls

**Seneca Falls Brewing Co.**
- 8491 Lower Lake Rd, Seneca Falls, NY 13148
- Great stop after the historical park

> **Good pairing:** Seneca Falls + drive south along Cayuga Lake Wine Trail on the way back.

## Finger Lakes Wine & Brewery Trail 🍷

### On Skaneateles Lake

**Anyela's Vineyards** ⭐ (only winery ON Skaneateles Lake)
- 2433 W Lake Rd, Skaneateles, NY 13152 (~10 min south of village)
- (315) 685-3797 | [anyelasvineyards.com](https://anyelasvineyards.com)
- 100 acres, 19 wines; estate-crafted reds, whites, spirits
- Stunning vineyard + lake views from patio; food/pizza on-site (verify availability)
- **Hours:** Mon–Sun 12pm–5pm (check for extended summer hours)
- Wine club available; Travelers' Choice winner on TripAdvisor

**Skaneateles Brewery**
- 34 Fennell St, Skaneateles, NY 13152 (historic mill building in village)
- Family-friendly, dog-friendly, live music + trivia nights
- Rotating craft taps, pub food
- Great for a casual evening with the whole group

### Cayuga Lake Wine Trail (~30–45 min west)

America's first wine trail — 16 wineries along Cayuga Lake. Easy to hit 2–4 in a half-day. Top picks for this trip:

- **King Ferry Winery / Treleaven Wines** — 658 Lake Rd, King Ferry NY 13081. Closest from Skaneateles (east shore); excellent whites.
- **Thirsty Owl Wine Company** — 6799 Elm Beach Rd, Ovid NY. Beautiful vineyard, excellent whites and reds.
- **Sheldrake Point Winery** — 7448 County Rd 153, Ovid NY. Stunning views, great rosé and Riesling.
- **Buttonwood Grove Winery** — 5986 NY-89, Romulus NY. Intimate and family-friendly.
- **Hosmer Winery** — 6999 NY-89, Ovid NY.

**Full trail map + passport:** [cayugawinetrail.com](https://cayugawinetrail.com)

> **Adult day strategy:** Maddie + Stephanie wine day → leave Egan with grandparent/babysitter → drive the west shore of Cayuga (NY-89), hit 3–4 wineries, lunch at one, circle back through Seneca Falls.

## Skaneateles Village Highlights 🏘️

**Clift Park**
- W Genesee St at the lakefront
- Gazebo, pier, benches, garden fountain
- Swimming area (opens late June, daily lifeguards 11am–7pm)
- Mid-Lakes Navigation boat tour dock
- No dogs allowed in park

**Mid-Lakes Navigation Cruises** (depart Clift Park)
- Sightseeing: $27 adults / $17 kids; 50 min; 7 days/week; dogs welcome on leash
- Lunch cruise: $56 adults / $34 kids; Tue/Wed/Fri 11am; starts June 1
- Dinner cruise: Fri/Sat 6pm, Sun 5pm; starts June 1
- U.S. Mail Boat: starts July 1 (Tuesdays) — unique experience, delivers mail to lake camps
- [midlakesnavigation.com](https://www.midlakesnavigation.com)

**Village Shopping & Strolling**
- Jordan St & W Genesee St: boutiques, galleries, antiques, home goods
- **Patisserie at the Sherwood Inn** — world-class pastries; grab a box for the road ⭐
- Skaneateles Antique Center
- Art galleries (check Skaneateles.com for current shows)

**Skaneateles Brewery**
- 34 Fennell St — rotating taps, pub food, live music; family/dog-friendly

**Austin Park**
- 1 E Austin St — covered picnic shelter, playground area (near farmers market)
- Reservable pavilion for group picnics
